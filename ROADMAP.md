# Product Roadmap — May–August 2026

## Month 1 — Validate (May 17 – June 17)

**Goal: Signal quality confidence before going public**

- [ ] Signal history log — append every signal daily with entry, stop, strategy, confidence. Starts the live track record clock.
- [ ] Paper trading — open a paper account, take every active signal the system generates. Logged, timestamped, public-ready.
- [ ] Tune BB profiles — 280 missing bear_breakdown profiles means short signals are running on defaults.
- [ ] Internal review — at end of month, audit signal outcomes. Win rate live vs backtest.

## Month 2 — Go Public (June 17 – July 17)

**Goal: Build FinTwit presence and public track record**

- [ ] Twitter account live — daily signal posts. Format: ticker, side, strategy, entry zone, stop, regime context.
- [ ] Post outcomes — when a signal closes, post the result. Win or loss. Public, timestamped.
- [ ] Signal history log public — simple table showing all signals and outcomes.
- [ ] KMR validation — 30+ days of live KMR signals. Compare live win rate vs backtest.
- [ ] Watchlist posts — near signals as daily Twitter watchlist. Drives engagement between active signals.

## Month 3 — Launch Prep (July 17 – August 17)

**Goal: External paid launch ready**

- [ ] HTML email output — convert scan to Beehiiv-compatible format
- [ ] Beehiiv setup — newsletter infrastructure at $20/mo
- [ ] Landing page — simple no-code first (Carrd). Signal philosophy, live track record, subscribe button.
- [ ] Twitter → newsletter funnel — bio link, pinned tweet with track record, CTA
- [ ] External beta — first 10–50 external subscribers. Founding member rate.

## Success Criteria at 3 Months

- 60+ days of logged live signals with documented outcomes
- Twitter account with consistent posting history and public track record
- First 10–50 external paid subscribers
- Live win rate defensibly close to backtest results
